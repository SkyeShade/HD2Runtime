# HD2Runtime 0.30.0 — Custom Stratagems

0.30.0 adds **custom stratagems**: mods register new stratagems with their own name, icon, code and payload. The
Runtime handles selection, carrier allocation, presentation, the beacon, every guarded write and every restore. The
release also adds weapon variants for every support weapon, armor stats and passives, Helldiver-wide fields, the
explosion and sound catalogues, the game's own HUD icons in mod overlays, and services for UI and editor mods.

What is live-tested is listed per capability in `docs/live-evidence.md` (`schemas/live_evidence.json`). Everything
else is validated offline, and its writes need `allow_unverified_effect` until a live test passes. The development
log of the 0.30.0 test builds (r1 to r59) is `docs/releases/0.30.0-dev.md`.

## Compatibility

- **Mods built for 0.28.x keep working.** No field that SDK 0.28.1 published as editable lost editability: 14,972
  fields then, 17,101 now, across the player weapon, support weapon, backpack and vehicle catalogs.
- **One new acknowledgement, handled as legacy.** The PLAS-45 Epoch's 30 partial-charge projectile, damage and
  explosion fields now need `allow_unverified_effect`, because only its partial charge fires that row. A mod that
  declares SDK 0.28.x or older is accepted without it, as a logged legacy operation, as in 0.28.1
  (`schemas/legacy_acknowledgements.json`, `docs/legacy-sdk-compatibility.md`). The 0.28.0 entries (146 fields) are
  unchanged.
- **Versions.** The startup line reads `HD2Runtime 0.30.0 initialized (API 1)`. A mod that uses anything new in 0.30.0
  declares `"min_version": "0.30.0"`. Mods built against the 0.30.0-dev test builds (`"0.30.0-dev"`) load too.
- **ModBuilder and other SDK tools.** The SDK publishes the new catalogues and schemas:
  - `CustomStratagemSchema.json` and `schemas/custom_stratagems.project.schema.json`;
  - `ArmorStatsCapabilities.json` and `ArmorPassiveCatalog.json`;
  - `HudIconSprites.json`, `ModelBaseCapabilities.json` and the sound and explosion catalogues;
  - the new field families in the existing capability files.

## Custom stratagems

`hd2.custom_stratagem.register({...})` (`docs/custom-stratagem-api.md`). A builder can write the same thing as data
(`custom_stratagems.json`, compiled by `hd2.py custom-stratagem compile`; `docs/custom-stratagem-builder.md`).

- **Selection.** A Runtime-owned panel beside the native loadout grid, in the native card look, with the game's FS
  Sinclair font, a details overlay (the code in arrows, call-in time, uses, cooldown), scrolling, and several instances
  of one definition.
- **The carrier is the stratagem.** A selected custom stratagem sits in a loadout slot as its carrier, a vanilla
  stratagem the player does not use that mission. Carriers are picked from dynamic pools, reserved for the lobby and
  blocked in the native picker while held. Carriers are never account items, and loadouts are never restricted.
- **Presentation.** Runtime-owned text and icons: the name, cased name, description, icon and call-in code in the
  HUD, the pickup prompt, the map and the panels. Everything is restored byte for byte aboard the ship.
- **Payload families:**
  - support pods (any support weapon or backpack, with per-launcher impact conversion, for example the Gas EAT);
  - expendable clones (an EAT-17 clone on an unused EAT-700 or EAT-411 type);
  - weapon variants;
  - orbital bombardment patterns and shell redirects;
  - Pelican close air support with native or custom guns;
  - Eagle rocket pods;
  - sentries;
  - the Shredder Silo;
  - script delivery.
- **Uses, cooldowns and traits per definition**, and teammates' custom stratagems shown on the teammate HUD.
- **Weapon variants for every support weapon.** `delivery={family='weapon'}` changes a support weapon's own type for
  one mission: its name and icon, its round (another output of its own compatibility class), and optionally a
  Runtime-owned model (`hd2.resources.model`).
  - Coverage: 34 of 35 support weapon types are hosts, and 32 register. The TX-41 Sterilizer's and the MS-11 Solo
    Silo's pods are not reviewed pickup racks; the SG-88 Break-Action has no stratagem of its own.
  - Hosts that world loot or another pod also brings need `allow_shared`.
- **Synced assets.** A peer on the same Runtime loads the packages another player's mod needs (the `hd2as` channel).

**Live-tested (solo host unless stated):**
- the panel, selection, virtual slots and their reconstruction;
- carrier conversion and presentation (custom text and icon, calldown codes, the slot overlay);
- the bombardment pattern and shell redirect;
- the expendable clone, its availability and payload;
- weapon variants:
  - the Maxigun with the Talon round and a debug model;
  - the AC-8 with the EAT-700's napalm round;
  - the GR-8 with the EAT-411's round;
  - the MG-206 with the APW-1's round;
  - the LAS-98 renamed.

Partial: the native-style panel; the carrier in the slot (two players, r43); the expendable delivery; the Pelican's
native gun; the Shredder Silo. See `docs/live-evidence.md`.

**Several players: EXPERIMENTAL.** Custom stratagems run in an experimental multiplayer scope. Every compatible Runtime
converts its own copy from the synced definition over the lobby channel (`hd2rt/1`). A mismatched registry fails
closed: custom stratagems are disabled lobby-wide and custom slots are locked. Live with two players:
- the Gas EAT (r3);
- four example mods with the carrier in the slot (r43).

The rest is not proven with several players. Custom projectiles are solo / host-only; see "Not in 0.30.0".

## Armor and the Helldiver

- **Armor passives** (`docs/armor-passives.md`): every passive of the game's 32 is named. The 411 armor kits are
  listed, 378 of them by name. A table says which passive effects follow a swap. Effect packages load before the
  write and sync to peers.
  - `hd2.player_passives.set({armor, second})` overrides the local player's armor passive, solo.
  - Live-proven armor swaps: SCOUT, ENGINEERING KIT, SERVO-ASSISTED, MED-KIT.
- **Armor stats** (`docs/armor-stats.md`, not live-tested): `hd2.armor_stats`.
  - Per kit: a kit's piece weights.
  - Per player, solo: the local player's armor bonus and stamina factor.
  - Every Helldiver on this machine: the per-weight class tables (armor value, speed, stamina) and the damage curve.
  - The class tables and the curve live in game.dll pages mapped PAGE_EXECUTE_READWRITE. They are written as
    **reviewed executable data**: exact 4-byte extents a domain registers after its own proofs, never re-protected,
    the only such exception (the user's decision). Whether an anti-cheat scan reacts to that is not known.
- **Type-wide Helldiver fields** (`hd2.helldiver()`, not live-tested): movement speeds, stamina, and each body zone's
  damage multiplier, durable share, damage to main health and health. These change every Helldiver this machine
  simulates, so every write needs `allow_shared`.

## Weapons, stratagems and the world

All of these are offline-proven unless `docs/live-evidence.md` says otherwise:
- muzzle, optics and underbarrel attachment stat modifiers (132 fields);
- the orbital bombardment pattern (salvos, shells, delays, scatter), `stratagem.call_in_time`, Eagle attack fields,
  and sentry and turret fields;
- PLAS-45 Epoch and RS-422 Railgun charge-level explosions;
- 32 more projectile donors (Eagle payloads, orbital shells, mortars, emplacements);
- `hd2.projectiles.homing`, and `hd2.projectiles.modify_shots` (per-shot stat changes on fired rounds), both solo;
- enemy spawn weights (`hd2.enemies.spawn_weight`);
- the explosion catalogue: 241 of 422 explosions named, editable, usable as payloads and requestable;
- vehicle tuning.

## Services for mods

- `hd2.ui.overlay` screen overlays, with Runtime fonts (FS Sinclair) and the game's own stratagem and booster HUD
  icons (`hd2.resources.game_icon`).
- Sounds: `hd2.sounds` (all 10,030 Wwise events catalogued) and playback controls.
- Input:
  - keys and chords;
  - the mouse wheel (experimental);
  - `hd2.input.block` (experimental).
- `hd2.on_frame`, a per-mod `store`, `hd2.build()` and `ensure` recovery.
- Editor services:
  - following choices;
  - every mod's options;
  - tuning custom stratagems.
- Diagnostics and performance:
  - per-mod CPU time ("which mod is slow");
  - a startup progress display;
  - faster initial discovery;
  - quieter logs.
- A version label aboard the ship.
- Proton and Wine: copy-on-write data pages are accepted as read contexts (GitHub issues #3 and #6; not live-tested on
  Linux).
- Building examples and mods from the downloads works from inside the extracted SDK (GitHub issue #2).

## Not in 0.30.0

- **Custom projectiles in multiplayer.** These are solo / host-only, and other players never see them:
  - Runtime-owned custom projectile rows;
  - projectile replacement;
  - the Pelican gun's custom rounds;
  - per-shot modification.

  They refuse `NOT_SOLO` or stay host-only with several players. The multiplayer design (each machine converting its
  own copy of a shot) moves to the next version. This was the user's decision of 2026-10-08.
- **Full weapon clones beyond the EAT class.** `scripts/research_clone_classes.py` reviewed every donor / carrier pair.
  Only EAT pairs are eligible; the others differ in their magazine contract, AI equipment type or backpack link, or
  have unreviewed members. A variant of the weapon itself is the supported customisation.
- **Composition with other writers.** A weapon variant refuses (`NOT_NATIVE`, nothing written) when another writer
  changed its records. The log names the changed words. Live: a third-party HMG buff mod.
