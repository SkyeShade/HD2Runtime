# BastionReArmoredRecreation

Recreates the BastionReArmored armored_16000 preset on the Bastion and Maelstrom: main health 8000 to 16000, every AP4 armor value (default zone and damage zones) to AP5, and the two stowage-bin ("lunch box") zones forwarding 0% instead of 100% of damage to main health. Zone targets are discovered through the API, not hard-coded offsets. Built only; never deployed or launched.
