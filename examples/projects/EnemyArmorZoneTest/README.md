# EnemyArmorZoneTest

Live test: Charger head armor 4 -> 1.

What to verify: shoot a newly spawned Charger in the head with a light-penetration primary (e.g. AR-23 Liberator).
Vanilla, armor 4 deflects those rounds. With this mod they should damage the head. The head is fatal and forwards 70%
of its damage to main health. Its other armor-4 plates stay vanilla, so they are the control.

Zone armor (DamageableZoneInfo +216) is gameplay-proven on vehicle zones. This test confirms the effect on enemies.
Built only.
